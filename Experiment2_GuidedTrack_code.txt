*page

	Thank you for taking part in this study! 

	The purpose of this study is to understand how people treat AIs and robots that are made up of different mind and body systems. To do this, we had some people read about robots that are controlled by a basic control system like a robot vacuum and some people read about robots controlled by an AI system like a large language model. These AI systems were defined either by mostly agentic capabilities like having goals or mostly emotive capabilities like expressing emotions. Some people read about systems that were partly agentic and partly emotive. Everyone then answered the same questions about all the same fictitious robots being developed at a fictitious company. The company and robot profiles were created for the purpose of this study but the robot and AI capabilities were inspired by real-life technologies. We hope that this survey will help us understand how people treat and think about the rights of robots and AIs with varying capabilities.
